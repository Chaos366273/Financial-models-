The ML-BL Rescue: Net Return With and Without the Overlay
averaged across the 3 overlay models · grey = unscaled (Table 3.10), green = with volatility overlay
Which volatility model you use barely matters. For every strategy, GARCH, GEW and LSTM-95 produce net returns within 0.2–1.0 percentage points of each other. LSTM-95 is consistently the most cautious (lowest net return, because it scales exposure down the hardest), GARCH is consistently the most aggressive (highest net return). The choice of overlay model is a minor tuning knob — not the thing driving results.
Realistic costs cut every strategy's net return roughly in half or worse. 1/N: 10.0%→7.1%. Min-Var: 12.9%→8.8%. ML-BL: 18.7%→5.0%. Top-50 Ensemble is hit hardest of all: 10.6%→1.6%, effectively wiped out. The overlay does not protect against turnover-driven costs — it only protects against volatility-driven drawdowns.
Bottom line: the overlay is what makes ML-BL the standout strategy under realistic costs (4.5–5.0% net, vs. near-zero or negative for the ranking books), but it cannot rescue a strategy whose edge was already eaten by trading costs. Min-Var remains the most "boring but reliable" option (8.3–8.8% net regardless of overlay model); Top-50 Ensemble is a strategy that only works in a zero-cost academic world.
