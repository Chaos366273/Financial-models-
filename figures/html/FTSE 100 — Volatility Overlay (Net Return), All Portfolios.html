The ML-BL Rescue: Net Return With and Without the Overlay
averaged across the 3 overlay models · grey = unscaled, green = with volatility overlay
ML-BL dominates every other portfolio, with or without the overlay. Even the two next-best strategies (Min-Var, MVO) top out around 12.5–12.9% net academic / 6.7–8.8% realistic — roughly a third of ML-BL's 18–18.7% / 4.5–5.0%. BL with no views underperforms MVO and Min-Var, showing the value of ML-generated views over a naive prior.
Which volatility model you use barely matters, for any strategy. Across all six portfolios, GARCH, GEW and LSTM-95 stay within roughly 0.2–1.0 percentage points of each other. LSTM-95 is consistently the most conservative (lowest net return everywhere), GARCH the most aggressive.
Realistic costs hit every strategy, but not equally. Top-50 Ensemble is hit hardest (10.6%→1.6%, essentially wiped out) since it trades the most names. MVO and BL (No Views) lose 35–40% of their academic return. ML-BL, despite its size, loses a smaller share proportionally (18.7%→5.0%, about a 73% cut vs Ensemble's 85% cut) because the overlay is actively protecting it from its own drawdowns.
